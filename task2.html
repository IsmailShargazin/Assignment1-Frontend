Welcome to AITU

This webpage contains simple and useful information for students of Astana IT University.

Here you can find study tips, campus information, and ideas for an active student life.

Study Tips

Attend your classes, take notes, complete your assignments on time, and ask your teachers questions.

Campus Information

The university has classrooms, study areas, a library, and places where students can relax.

Student Life

Students can join university clubs, participate in events, play sports, and spend time with friends.
